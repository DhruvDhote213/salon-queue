# Salon Queue — Brief

## Problem
On weekends customers sit 1–2 hours in the salon without knowing their turn. Some leave, and others argue about who came first. The salon needs a fair, visible queue.

## Users
- Customers (join and track from their phone)
- Receptionist / owner (manage the queue, add walk-ins, change settings)
- Barbers (start and finish services)

## Must-have features
1. Join the queue from your phone: pick 1–3 services and a barber (or "Any")
2. Live status: position in line, estimated wait, "Start coming" notice, and a full-screen "It's your turn!" when called
3. Reception dashboard: add walk-ins, call next for a barber, mark no-show, cancel
4. Barber screen: current customer, Start / Done, On break toggle
5. Waiting-area TV: who is with which barber and the next 5 tokens
6. Owner settings: services on/off, barbers on duty, open/close online joining, today's stats
7. English and Gujarati labels with a language toggle
8. Shared PostgreSQL database so phone, tablet and TV stay in sync

## Don't build
Online payment, future-date appointments, SMS/WhatsApp, loyalty programme, multiple branches, staff login/PIN (not built in this version).

## Look & feel
Premium salon feel: dark charcoal with a gold accent, clean, mobile-first.
